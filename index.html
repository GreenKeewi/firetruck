YOUR FIRST SHIFT

Keep it smooth.

01 · Drive the truck

W / ↑ accelerates. A D / ← → steer progressively. S / ↓ brakes to zero, then reverses after a brief hold. Space gives full braking. Shift plus the pedals helps with parking. Slow down before turning the long truck.

02 · Respond

Follow the amber route to the current incident. J toggles the siren. Drivers react individually after 1–2 seconds and pull aside only when safe. Emergency signal priority clears cross traffic before showing green. Traffic and trucks can block the road. The response clock runs only during an active call.

03 · Park the whole truck

Match the bay arrow, put the complete body inside the outline, then hold still for 1.5 seconds. Q/E glance left/right; hold C for a rearward view. R returns to a safe recent position when one is clear.

04 · Between calls

Take 15–30 seconds to reset the crew, or press Enter to accept the next dispatch sooner. Esc/P pauses. Fire calls: after parking, press F for the roof monitor. Arrows aim, Space sprays and Shift gives fine aim; F returns to driving. Extinguish all flames to finish the call. Three missed calls or an empty condition gauge ends the run. Esc → Restart run starts again.
